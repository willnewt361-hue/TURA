Frequently asked questions

How do I book a ticket?

Sign in, search your route and date, choose a departure, pick a seat, enter passenger details and complete the mock checkout in this demo build. Your e-ticket appears immediately.

Do I need an account?

Yes in this build. Guest booking by reference and phone is planned for a later release; until then bookings are tied to the signed-in account.

Which payment methods are accepted?

This build uses a mock payment provider. No mobile-money wallet or card is charged. The MTN MoMo integration boundary exists, but live payments are disabled until configured.

How long are my seats held?

Seats are locked for a short window (120 seconds by default) while you check out. Expired locks are released automatically.

How do I board?

Arrive early with your ID and show the QR code on your ticket. Ticket validation is online-only and each ticket can be used once.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Cancellation and refunds are not enabled in this demo. Contact the operator for real-world changes once live operations begin.

I paid but didn't get a ticket

Open My tickets. If it still does not appear, keep your payment reference and contact support. In this demo, payments are simulated and can be retried safely.

Is the tracking live?

Not yet. Trip progress is a simulated corridor tracker advanced by the driver or operator, not vehicle GPS.
