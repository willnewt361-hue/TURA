Verify a ticket

Paste the ticket token from a QR code, or open this page with ?token=…. This check is read-only: it does not mark the ticket used. A driver or operator still validates the ticket at boarding.

How verification works

Signed token

Each QR contains an HMAC-SHA256 signed payload. Editing the token invalidates the signature.

Single use

Only a driver or operator can validate a ticket, and a validated ticket cannot be reused.

No personal data

Verification never returns a passenger phone number or email address.
