Terms of service

1. Service

TURA lets travellers search, reserve and pay for intercity bus seats operated by independent bus companies. The transport contract is between you and the operator.

2. Bookings & payment

Seats are held for a short window pending payment. A booking is confirmed only when payment succeeds and an e-ticket is issued. This demo uses simulated payments.

3. Cancellations

Online cancellation is not enabled in this build. In a live deployment, refunds follow the operator's policy and may take several business days.

4. Travel

Arrive early with a valid ID. Operators may refuse boarding for invalid tickets or conduct that endangers others.

5. Liability

TURA is not liable for delays, cancellations or incidents caused by the operator or by events outside our control, to the extent permitted by law.

6. Demo limits

Payments are mocked, trip tracking is simulated, and SOS alerts stay inside the operations dashboard. Do not treat this build as a live emergency or payment system.

Privacy policy

1. What we collect

Passenger names, phone number, optional email, booking and payment status. We do not store card numbers or mobile-money PINs.

2. How we use it

To issue tickets, show trip details, prevent fraud, provide support and improve the service.

3. Sharing

Passenger names and seat numbers are shared with the operator running your trip. Payment data is shared with the payment provider. We do not sell personal data.

4. Retention

Booking records are kept while needed for operations, accounting and dispute resolution. Offline ticket copies stay in your browser until you sign out or clear site data.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS in production and stored in an access-controlled database. Sessions use signed, HTTP-only cookies.

6. Children

The service is intended for travellers who can enter a transport contract. A parent or guardian should book for minors.

Your data rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data by contacting support. We aim to comply with the Uganda Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and equivalent laws where applicable.

To protect passengers, ticket QR codes contain a signed reference but no phone number or password. Ticket validation happens on the server and each ticket is single-use.
