System requirements

What you need to run TURA in the real world. This build is a deployable V1 foundation: review the limitations at the end before inviting passengers.

Hosting & infrastructure

ItemMinimumNotes
Application server1 vCPU · 1 GB RAM · 10 GB SSDSingle Uvicorn worker while using SQLite. Add RAM before scaling.
DatabaseSQLite file on a persistent diskOne worker and one instance only. Move to PostgreSQL before horizontal scaling.
BackupsDaily copy off the serverBackups are admin-only and disabled in demo mode. Test a restore every quarter.
NetworkDomain + HTTPS certificateSet SESSION_COOKIE_SECURE=true and leave CORS_ORIGINS empty for same-origin hosting.

Bus park / booking office

ItemSpecificationNotes
Counter computer4 GB RAM · modern Chrome, Edge, Firefox or SafariUsed by agents and managers.
Boarding deviceAndroid 8+ phone or tablet with rear cameraScan the QR from the ticket. Validation is online-only.
Internet5 Mbps+ per counter, 4G backupBoarding verification and live updates need a connection.
PowerUPS or small solar kit for router + counterLoad-shedding is the most common cause of park downtime.
Printer (optional)58/80 mm thermal printerTickets also print from the browser.

Known limitations

Payments

Simulated. No wallet or card is charged.

Tracking

Simulated corridor waypoints, not vehicle GPS.

SOS

Internal operations alert only. Never contacts emergency services.

Offline

Cached tickets and itinerary are read-only and may be stale. Validation needs a connection.

Scaling

In-memory rate limiter and WebSocket rooms require one instance.

Migrations

No automated database migrations yet. Back up before upgrading.
