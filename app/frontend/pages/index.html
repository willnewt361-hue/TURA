    Questions, answered.

    What is TURA?

    TURA is a bus travel app for Uganda. You search routes, compare buses, pick a seat and view your ticket.

    Do I need an account?

    You can open the app and look around first. Booking and tickets use your account in the connected app.

    Can I pay in the app?

    Not yet. Version 1 shows a payment preview only, and no real payment is taken.

    Will my ticket work at the bus?

    Not in this demo. The preview QR code is not valid for boarding.

    Is trip tracking live?

    No. Tracking screens are illustrative in this version.
